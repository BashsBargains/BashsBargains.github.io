Coupon 101

How stacking works

Most stores let you use one store coupon and one manufacturer coupon on the same item, on top of the sale price. Rebate apps like Ibotta and Fetch pay you back after you shop, so they stack with everything.

ExtraBucks & Register Rewards

These are store credit, not cash. CVS ExtraBucks and Walgreens Register Rewards print on your receipt and come off your next purchase. "Pay" on each card is what you hand the cashier; the big yellow price is what it costs after the rewards come back.

Roll your rewards

The pro move: use the rewards from deal #1 to pay for deal #2, then those rewards on deal #3. Start with the deal that pays back the most so you're spending store credit instead of cash.

Before you go
  • Scan your store card or app first. Most sale prices need it.
  • Check the limit. Many rewards deals are once per week per card.
  • Grab the exact size listed. A different size usually won't trigger the deal.
  • Keep your receipt until the rebate app pays out.
