Save on delivery

Before you order

Check the app's own Offers tab. DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub all show deals picked for you there.

Try the restaurant's own app too. Deals like Shake Shack's free burger work on the restaurant's own delivery but not on DoorDash or Uber Eats.

Watch the fees. Menu prices on delivery apps are often higher than in the restaurant, so a code doesn't always mean a deal.
