Deals for RTs 🫁

Happy Respiratory Care Week, my fellow RTs! (Oct 25–31, 2026.) From one RT to another: thank you for every vent check, every neb, every code you ran to and every breath you helped someone take. We don't always get the spotlight, so I rounded up 17 free CE offers and discounts we actually qualify for. Treat yourself. You deserve it. 💛

Before you buy: deals change fast, so open the source and confirm the offer first. Most of these need you to verify (ID.me, SheerID, your NPI number or a work badge/license), so set that up once and you're good for most of the list. Everything here is free to use and links straight to where I found it.

Free CE for RC Week

  • Sentec

    Oct 26–30, 2026 (live)

    Free live CE webinars for Respiratory Care & Sleep Technologist Appreciation Week

    How to get it
    Pick a webinar on Sentec's free CEU page and hit Register. There are on-demand courses too if you can't make it live. Check each course's listing for its CE credit.

    Source: sentec.com →

  • Tri-anim

    Through Oct 29, 2026

    Free continuing education all October, each program approved for 1.0 CRCE

    How to get it
    Register from Tri-anim's RC Week page. Grab the last few before they wrap up.

    Source: tri-anim.com →

  • continued (Respiratory Therapy)

    Try a free course, no credit card needed. Unlimited AARC-approved CE is $99/year after that.

    How to get it
    Sign up for the free course on continued's respiratory therapy page.

    Source: continued.com →

  • Respiratory Therapy Zone

    Free access to 15+ RRT practice courses and quizzes

    How to get it
    Use the “Get Free Access” box on their RC Week guide. Great if you're studying for boards or have a new grad on your team.

    Source: respiratorytherapyzone.com →

Made for RTs (yes, us by name)

  • Verizon

    Nurses & RTs discount: $10 off a month with 1 line, $25 off a month with 2 lines on Unlimited Ultimate, Plus or Welcome (10% off per line on the Simplicity plan)

    How to get it
    Verify through ID.me as a Medical provider using your NPI number, then register on Verizon's Nurses discounts page. You need to be currently employed as an RT. Re-verify about once a year.

    Source: verizon.com →

Clothes & shoes

  • adidas

    30% off online, in stores and at factory outlets

    How to get it
    Verify with ID.me. Hospital and health-system employees qualify with proof of employment. 2 uses a month online, 1 a month in store. Some popular styles (Samba, Gazelle, Ultraboost and others) are excluded.

    Source: adidas.com →

  • Calvin Klein

    Extra 20% off select items, one order

    How to get it
    Verify with ID.me (you may need to upload your license). Single-use code, can't be combined with other promos.

    Source: calvinklein.us →

  • J.Crew & J.Crew Factory

    Extra 15% off, in stores and online

    How to get it
    Fill out the health-care worker form (you may need to upload a document). The offer lands in your J.Crew Passport wallet a day later. Bring ID in store.

    Source: factory.jcrew.com →

  • Madewell

    15% off for medical professionals

    Source: shop.sheerid.com →

  • Crocs

    Source: crocs.com →

  • Birkenstock
