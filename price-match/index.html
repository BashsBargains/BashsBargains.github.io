Price Match Cheat Sheet 🤝

Found it cheaper somewhere else? Here's which stores will match the lower price, how long after you buy, and exactly what to show at the register. Heads up: Target and Walmart don't match other stores anymore.

How to get the match

  1. Same exact item. Brand, model number, size and color all have to match.
  2. Sold by the store itself. Amazon or Walmart Marketplace sellers don't count.
  3. Screenshot the proof with the price, the web address and "in stock" showing.
  4. Ask before you pay. It's easier than asking for a refund later.
  5. Bought it and the price dropped? Check the "after you buy" window below. Costco gives you 30 days!
