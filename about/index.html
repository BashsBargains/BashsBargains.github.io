About Bash's Bargains

Hi, I'm Bash 👋 By day I'm a respiratory therapist. By night (and apparently between patients) I'm a deal hunter.

How it started

Deals that cost money aren't deals

This whole thing started with my inbox. I was finally chipping away at my 30,000 unread emails when I found one from a "deal finder" site bragging about an incredible clearance find… that I'd have to pay to unlock. Excuse me?

So that same night I sat down with Claude, an AI assistant, and we built our own. No paywall, no sign-up, no catch. Just clearance finds and grocery deals, rounded up every morning for anyone who wants them.

It's a side project I'm genuinely proud of, and it's only getting better.

What's here

Free deals, rounded up for you

Clearance finds and price drops from the big stores, grocery deals and dinner ideas for Delaware and the Philly area, coupon matchups for CVS, Walgreens and local grocery stores, and a Black Friday planner. No sign-up, no paywall.

How deals are picked

Real savings only

Deals are gathered from store ads, retailer pages and well-known deal and couponing sites, with help from AI, and checked against a second source when possible. I only post a price when it's written out somewhere I can verify, and every deal links to where it came from.

Prices change fast and can differ by store and ZIP code, so always confirm the price before you buy.
